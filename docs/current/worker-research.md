# Worker research and escalation

Use GPT-6 Luna/high for autonomous function/family research as well as routine grinding. CHEAP, MEDIUM and SUPERVISOR describe workflow eligibility, not required model intelligence. Keep production source/manifest promotion serialized through the existing grinder and fresh whole-image acceptance.

## Production and research bounds

The ordinary production attempt budget and interval blockers still apply. Separately authorized, bounded research may investigate blocked functions in isolated directories without reopening production. Record its authorization, compiler-process ceiling and meaningful-iteration ceiling before starting. Research output cannot promote itself, redefine boundaries, trim objects or authorize unsupported binding. A production reopen still needs concrete new evidence or a tested capability.

State whether per-case allocations are hard limits or merely an initial distribution of a shared ceiling, and name who may reallocate unused slots. Do not ask for renewed permission when that authority is already granted. Record reallocations before the next experiment. Missing evidence and exhausted allocations remain separate stop reasons.

Within that research scope, the worker may move from source shape to types/signedness, locals/parameters, ABI/declarations, caller/callee evidence, TU/compiler context and mapping/binding evidence without requesting advice for each step. Preserve full object/fixup evidence. Inspect compact diagnostics first, with access to the same full evidence and historical negative trials. After two hypotheses collapse to the same effective output, change the analysis level. A changed source hash or object metadata alone is not recovery progress.

Continue while supported experiments discriminate among explanations. Stop a line when exactness is established, a concrete unsupported capability is demonstrated, or materially distinct evidence-supported hypotheses are exhausted. A shared experiment ceiling can censor the investigation earlier: record that explicitly, including remaining supported questions. Count precompiler failures separately from actual compiler processes; record mandatory verification recompiles separately from hypothesis experiments.

## Compact task and handoff

Give a worker the function/family identity, precise objective, evidence paths, authorization boundaries, remaining budget and acceptance requirements. Let it retrieve and own the detailed context. Archive sources, predictions, falsifiers, compiler outcomes and full diagnostics locally; do not relay every iteration through the supervisor.

Escalate only a decision-relevant summary, normally no more than 250 words plus a small table:

- Current verified state and strict acceptance status.
- Materially distinct hypotheses and unique effective compiler outcomes.
- Concrete facts and hypotheses ruled out, with scope.
- Exact remaining blocker and smallest relevant artifact paths.
- Next genuinely discriminating question and required capability or evidence.

Omit routine tool chatter, repeated observations and chronological narration. The supervisor should return concise constraints or a high-leverage question, not prescribe every local source edit. Long source/assembly dumps belong in referenced artifacts and are read only when needed for a particular decision.

## Capability and tooling audit

For an unresolved case, record whether more meaningful attempts remain; necessary evidence and prior trials were accessible; multiple discriminating variants could compile; diagnostics exposed the mismatch; higher-level ABI/TU/mapping research was permitted; and a production gate prevented only mutation or also investigation. Budget stops, missing tools, stale packets and unavailable evidence are not model-capability failures.

Before attributing a later Astra success to intelligence, identify its extra evidence, tools, freedoms and experiments. Supply transferable advantages to Luna first when cheap and safe. A capability comparison requires comparable starting evidence, permissions, tools and work allowances. Otherwise report an operational outcome, not a controlled model comparison.

Audit friction in the complete hypothesis → compile → structured diff → interpretation → next experiment → archive loop. Fix a demonstrated recurring obstacle at its smallest useful scope; do not build an evaluation platform or add diagnostics speculatively. Preserve the current diagnostic feature freeze until such evidence justifies a change.

## Economics

Account for all participating models, including supervisor input, cached input, output and handoff/context overhead. Reasoning tokens are part of output. Report actual usage separately from API-equivalent price scenarios; absent billing metadata, do not claim invoice costs. Normalize progress by total model-weighted cost, with strict recovered bytes/functions separate from diagnostic narrowing. Do not infer avoided Astra work or causal efficiency gains without a comparable baseline.
