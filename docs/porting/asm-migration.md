# ASM migration inventory

This inventory separates historical ASM ownership from SDL3 port work. Owner rows preserve full manifest extents; routine rows carry the available entry semantics, platform dependencies, replacement direction, first-link requirement, confidence, and evidence. Mixed owners stay mixed at the owner level and are split by routine for port planning. The [JSON companion](asm-migration.json) retains full evidence and exports.

Categories: `PLATFORM` (DOS/device/runtime boundary), `LEGACY_SOFTWARE_RENDERER` (software raster and indexed rendering), `PORTABLE_ALGORITHM` (portable math/state/data logic requiring a faithful translation), and `HISTORICAL_CRT-DEAD-NOP` (pinned CRT or verified no-op/dead entries retained for historical coverage).

**Audio boundary:** the game calls loaded audio drivers through far-call vectors; the audio-driver interface is not INT 60h-62h. See [audio drivers](audio-drivers.md).

Completeness is checked against every `MATCHING_ASM`, `MATCHING_ASM_DATA`, `KNOWN_TOOLCHAIN_LIBRARY`, and `KNOWN_TOOLCHAIN_LIBRARY_DATA` row in `layout/manifest.json`, plus every `runtime_data_members` entry.

## Mixed-family owners and unresolved boundaries

Owners with `category: MIXED` contain routines from more than one class; the owner is not a single porting unit. Translate each public routine under its own category while preserving complete owner coverage until an explicit boundary or replacement is proven. `mat_invert` and `sprite_make_wnd` retain the F4c boundary/structure caveats; `zclipline` and `nopsub_326BA` retain their semantic uncertainty. These uncertainties do not justify dropping bytes or inventing behavior.
