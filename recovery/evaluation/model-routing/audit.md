The current guidelines correctly require matched evidence, tools, permissions and meaningful budget, and warn that workflow gates are not model limits.

Treat it as a research replay candidate, not another verified same-trajectory solve. `toupper` and `parse_shape2d_helper3` were already exact at their first archived candidate; the other promotion-only tasks lack failing seeds. Keep these as controls, not search cases.
