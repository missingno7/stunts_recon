# Broader ordinary-C cohort: results and limits

Authority: local research receipts plus the repository's native promotion and
validation records. Initial baseline was 26 strict game-C functions / 1,118 B;
queue 0 CHEAP / 7 MEDIUM / 576 SUPERVISOR. The shared research ceiling was
256 actual hypothesis compiler processes, initially 64 per task. It was a
ceiling, not a target. The fifth task was allocated 16 from unused
`mat_multiply` capacity before its first compiler process; see `ledger.json`.

## Selection and reusable preparation

`screening.md` records the original four suggestions, two replacements, and
the follow-on. `init_rect_arrays` was not ready: the new focused packet exposed
an unregistered DGROUP address previously hidden by triage's early return.
`file_decomp_rle_seq` begins inside an enclosing near-procedure frame, so an
independent far-function source search would be misleading. The packet utility
verifies current card/workflow and immutable oracle identities, decodes the
complete interval, and reports flow, calls, relocations, memory operands and
fresh triage obligations. It is research-only; it does not authorize promotion.
It passed six initial current-card checks and unknown/stale negative checks.
The subsequent 41-card screen found four additional >220-byte intervals with
no newly exposed triage blocker, of which the 260-byte `mat_mul_vector` had
the smallest independently evidenced semantic lead. This remains bounded
screening, not an origin or TU proof.

Preparation cost included the initial full validation; nine context/extent
reviews for the original/replacement shortlist; complete source/ASM/ABI
review for the selected tasks; focused packet checks for six initial and 41
further cards; and one more full context/extent review for `mat_mul_vector`.
The per-task source-search allocations begin after that work. Earlier
`file_get_shape2d` history comprises at least three source compiler processes
and four version-profile contrasts, separate from this pilot.

### Strict rectangle recovery

The first nested early-return hypothesis gave a full 130-byte extent and
46/57 exact instructions. Its six mismatch islands were field displacements.
The original `RECTANGLE` member order is left/right/top/bottom. Correcting
that declaration, without a compiler-profile change, gave literal byte
identity at hypothesis 7; hypothesis 8's comparison-order change regressed.
The exact archived object has one public at offset zero, no fixups and no
private data. A reviewed pristine instruction/CFG overlay covers 57
instructions, 55 reachable, two unreachable alignment NOPs, no calls or MZ
relocations. The native grinder returned FAST_PASS_ONLY and then PROMOTED;
`python tools/validate.py` independently checked all 27 active C functions,
179 tests and the whole HYBRID_EXACT image. Current strict game-C ownership is
27 functions / 1,248 B, queue 0 CHEAP / 7 MEDIUM / 575 SUPERVISOR. This does
not identify a unique historical source or original TU.

### Unresolved dimensions

`mat_multiply`: semantically exact long C multiplication consistently calls
the `__aFlmul` runtime helper, absent from the 128-byte pristine interval.
Narrow signed-int controls emit inline IMUL but discard the high half required
by the target. Exact helper-free C limb forms emitted 702–718 B; public helper
forms added another public and near fixups. This is a scoped source/compiler
expressibility cost result, not proof of assembly origin or a different
historical compiler. Useful strict results after hypotheses 3/8/16/32:
0/0/0/not reached. Detailed receipts and per-round costs are in
`build/private/cohort-mat-multiply/summary.md`.

`heapsort_by_order`: useful results occurred well after candidate 16. The
best, hypothesis 33, used fixed struct-local slots and matched 44/66 aligned
instructions with 66.67% target anchoring. Its complete single-public,
zero-fixup code is 192 B, still 42 B over the target; repeated address setup
accounts for 15 extra instructions. The next two focused lifetime/address
contrasts lost anchors. No candidate reached the required complete extent.
At candidates 3/8/16/32, no strict solve; candidate 33 was the best late
diagnostic result. The 64-process share was not exhausted (36 used).

`file_get_shape2d`: 26 compiler processes produced 25 distinct outputs;
the final controlled `register` formal contrast collapsed to identical 124 B
code with a 14-byte BP frame and counted shifts. No tested form reproduced
the target's compact ES:SI lifetime or 95-byte extent. Restunts' far-pointer
formula is semantic context and differs from pristine ES:[BX]/ES×16 behavior.
Historical source/TU and complete caller evidence are missing; the production
interval block remains. No strict result at candidates 3/8/16 or 24, and
candidate 32 was not reached. The 64-process share was not exhausted (26 used).

`mat_mul_vector` follow-on: its 260-byte original independently shows nine
guarded signed IMUL high-product operations. Restunts-style unrolled long C
emitted 508–516 B and nine `__aFlmul` fixups. Loop forms shrank to 204/222 B
but kept three static helper-call sites and different topology. Exact
helper-free, fully unrolled limb forms emitted 1,964/2,008 B. This confirms
the same *tested source/compiler lowering signature* in a second matrix
function without proving ASM origin or excluding all C/compiler contexts.
Seven processes over two rounds were sufficient for that bounded question;
candidate 3 had no strict solve and later 8/16/32 thresholds were not reached.
One modern anonymous-union form was rejected by MSC after launch. Detailed
receipts are in `build/private/cohort-mat-mul-vector/summary.md`.

## Proof and cost boundary

Research batches did not spend production attempts. The rectangle had one
production attempt rejected before compilation for missing reviewed packet,
then one native FAST attempt and one serialized promotion. The latter freshly
compiled its candidate plus the staged and canonical whole-image builds.
The local ledger records **at least 216 active-C verification compiler
processes** across initial validation, rectangle FAST/promotion, the immediate
post-promotion validation and final handoff validation, separate from cohort
hypothesis compiles. This lower bound excludes
any compiler processes inside tests; full builds recompile every active C
owner, so counting only the rectangle candidate would understate total cost.

Luna/high workers performed isolated function research, one Sol/high worker
built the focused packet, and the root handled screening, accounting, overlay,
promotion and validation. Per-worker token/billing metadata is unavailable;
therefore strict bytes per total model-weighted cost cannot be computed and
no causal speedup over the earlier workflow is claimed. The pilot establishes
that broader selection and adaptive batches can produce a strict >80-byte
recovery (130 B in eight hypothesis compiler processes), while three other
well-bounded lines exposed concrete source, compiler or evidence limits.
Meaningful late heapsort behavior at process 33 shows why a fixed three-try
rule would have hidden diagnostic progress, but its near match is not accepted.

Native acceptance remains exact whole-image/oracle with complete contribution,
public/fixup and relocation obligations. No compiler output was trimmed,
patched, byte-embedded, or promoted on similarity. All retained research
objects and logs live under ignored `build/private`; production source,
recipe, evidence overlay and promotion receipts are tracked normally.
