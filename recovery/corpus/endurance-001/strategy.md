# Recovery strategy after endurance-001

Use longer-lived Luna/high workers for isolated function/family investigation, including SUPERVISOR cases. Keep production promotion serialized and strict. Reserve Astra for a concrete unresolved decision or a shared capability question after the worker has compressed its evidence. This is a practical allocation decision supported by cost and observed research behavior, not proof that extended Luna search improves accepted recovery per dollar.

## Recovery and research are different outcomes

The initial routine batch processed seven cases with ten hypothesis compiler processes and promoted `toupper` (24 bytes). The endurance sample then investigated ten unresolved cases and carried two existing exact controls without recompiling them. Its 31 compiler processes produced 21 new effective output identities, eight repeats and two compiler syntax rejections; two precompiler harness failures are separate. Endurance added **zero strict recoveries**. Combined hypothesis processes: 41 of the authorized 50. Mandatory promotion/validation recompiles are outside that hypothesis count.

Per-case hypothesis bands 1–3 / 4–6 / 7–10 / 11+ produced 14 / 5 / 2 / 0 new effective outputs and zero endurance promotions in every band. Output novelty is evidence about the search space, not acceptance or proof of useful progress. The selected small sample, inherited histories and unequal initial allocations do not establish an optimal retry budget or minimum intelligence level.

Luna did move beyond source spelling: it investigated types, compiler context, ABI, symbol fixups, segment declarations and same-TU ordering. It bypassed a production include gate safely in isolation. It identified B4/LEXTDEF records in three objects across two cases, then used the existing generic OMF reader and frozen diagnostics to expose complete records/fixups. Strict production parsing still rejects these records. The same-TU call geometry is a supported research observation, not an exact linked contribution; multi-contribution binding remains unverified.

## Constraints and tooling

An initial allocation mistake nearly stopped a supported same-TU experiment despite unused shared budget. Clarifying already-granted reallocation authority let Luna perform it. That is a worker-environment failure, not evidence that Astra source reasoning was necessary. Some other cases remain censored by their allocation or by missing original declaration/caller/TU evidence. Nine shared slots remained unused; do not call this exhaustive search or a global budget stop.

The repeated tooling obstacle was strict static-symbol parsing. The generic diagnostic reader already supplies a safe research path; no new diagnostic mechanism was needed. A production extension would require visibility-preserving B4/B6 handling and independently verified binding, not merely suppressing a rejection. Based-segment syntax rejected by MSC 5.10 is a separate compiler-generation boundary. Both are more precise than a model-capability label. Full sources, predictions, falsifiers, outcomes and limitation scopes remain in the case artifacts and `handoff.md`.

No matched Astra cohort was run. Astra supplied orchestration, budget clarification and acceptance review, not a later source solution. Consequently this work neither proves an Astra capability advantage nor measures what Astra-from-start would have cost. Before such a comparison, normalize evidence, tools, permissions and meaningful experiment allowance; transfer cheap environmental advantages to Luna first.

## Economics and operating policy

Published same-tier API component rates differ by 100×; see `../batch-001/pricing-reference.json`. Actual model token records, including supervisor input and output, are in `../batch-001/usage.json`. These are API-equivalent scenarios, not subscription charges. Cached input is counted at its own rate, reasoning is already included in output, and unavailable billing/model rates remain unavailable. The timestamped checkpoint includes corpus setup, triage, research, supervision and reporting, not just successful recovery.

The measured supervisor share dominates this batch's modeled cost. Reducing routine Astra involvement therefore has stronger direct economic support than demanding fewer Luna compiles. No avoided supervisor tokens or causal efficiency gain are claimed. Use compact bounded assignments, local archives and decision-only escalation; repeated supervisor polling, large handoffs and re-onboarding also cost money.

The durable rules are in `docs/current/worker-research.md`. Continue supported discriminating research across analysis levels; do not spend slots on cosmetic repeats. Keep model capability, missing evidence, workflow gates and production safety separate. Improve tooling only for a demonstrated obstacle. Current evidence supports broader **research autonomy**, while recovery-speed benefit and the best stopping budget remain unproven.
