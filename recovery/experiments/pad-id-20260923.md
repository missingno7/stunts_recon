# `pad_id` bounded C lowering research

Pristine `[0x16C06,0x16C3C)` is 54 bytes, SHA-256
`d879bc71b56c3a930f733223417abea782e3ce9081c7974ab169a90d78ae25d0`.
Its 21 complete decoded instructions copy four bytes from a far pointer to
DS:`6AD2`–`6AD5`, terminate at DS:`6AD6`, replace zero bytes in the first
four positions with spaces, and return the near buffer pointer. Restunts
`seg007.asm:138-170` labels the destination as `word_42242`, `word_42244`,
and `byte_42246`; `dseg.asm:25616-25618` declares these adjacent objects.
Those names and the DGROUP frame are source/address evidence, but the
production binder was not extended because no source form matched.

Two independent source hypotheses and falsifiers were frozen under
`build/private/research-plans/pad-id-20260923/manifest.json` before two
MSC 5.10 compiler processes. The long-copy form emitted 64 bytes, paired
AX/DX far loads and word stores, but used a BP-2 loop index. The explicit
word-copy form also emitted 64 bytes and changed the load/store shape.
These are two distinct effective outputs, both with unresolved ordinary
DGROUP offset16 fixups. The original objects and full diagnostics are local
under `build/private/research-batches/pad_id/0a327969f864/`.

The next one-process plan predicted that `register int i` would choose SI
and remove the stack frame. It selected SI and the target loop shape, but
retained `sub sp,2` and emitted a trailing NOP, producing 58 bytes. A
separate one-process MSC 5.00 comparison emitted the same 58 code bytes and
ordered fixups, so this frame/pad difference is not explained by those two
pinned compiler versions. Plans, source, original objects/logs, and full
diagnostics are local under
`build/private/research-plans/pad-id-register-20260923/` and
`build/private/research-plans/pad-id-msc500-20260923/`.

Three distinct source forms and four research compiler processes were used;
there was no production source attempt or promotion. The exact C or TU
mechanism for an SI loop without the two-byte register home is unresolved.
Do not force a match by trimming the object or ignoring the NOP/fixups.
